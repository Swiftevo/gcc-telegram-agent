## Sanitization Boundary

This evidence layer preserves facts needed to understand the application and its
governance risks. It deliberately excludes voter names, wallet addresses,
per-voter timestamps, per-voter voting power, named reviewer attribution,
unnecessary personal biographies, and meeting or recording credentials.

All statements below are applicant-, proposal-, or discussion-reported unless a
separate source is named. They are not independent technical validation and are
not evidence of payment, milestone acceptance, unlock, or completed delivery.

## Project and Public Problem

OpenRPC is presented as an Apache 2.0 open standard for describing JSON-RPC APIs
in machine- and human-readable form. The application compares its role to an
OpenAPI or Swagger layer designed specifically for JSON-RPC.

The proposal identifies three public infrastructure problems:

- fragmented documentation, specification drift and repeated integration work;
- reliability and security risks where wallets, nodes, SDKs and applications
  depend on ambiguous JSON-RPC interfaces;
- a high developer-integration burden without standardized specifications and
  reusable validation or generation tools.

## Source-Reported Adoption and Current State

The proposal reports more than six years of ecosystem use, more than 5.9 million
TypeScript or NPM toolkit downloads in the preceding year, and adoption across
multiple blockchain infrastructure projects. It describes an existing tool
ecosystem that includes a playground, documentation generation, client code
generation, client and server libraries, and testing or validation tools.

These figures and adopter claims remain source-reported. This import does not
independently reproduce package-registry queries or verify each integration.

## Proposed Contribution to Chinese-Speaking Developers

The application argues that structured specifications can reduce reliance on
natural-language translation and make APIs from Chinese-speaking projects easier
for global developers to validate and integrate. It also argues that
Chinese-speaking developers can integrate global JSON-RPC APIs with fewer
translation ambiguities and that structured specifications can improve the
reliability of code-generation tools and AI assistants.

During public discussion, multilingual document generation and direct connection
with Chinese-speaking developer communities were discussed. The project expressed
interest in helping Chinese-speaking JSON-RPC projects standardize their APIs and
increase international reach. These discussion points were not added to the two
formal funded milestones in the captured proposal.

## Funding and Sustainability Context

The proposal reports 175,000 USD in commitments from three ecosystem funders in
the preceding year and says the project was exploring other grants and
investment. It states that the core standard and tools would remain Apache 2.0
open source.

An optional future sustainability path described in the proposal is hosted
services for documentation publishing, API usage monitoring or team
collaboration. This is a proposed business model, not evidence of current revenue.

## Delivery Capacity

and experience across developer tooling, wallets, infrastructure, consulting and

duplicated in this sanitized evidence because the evaluation-relevant fact is the
claimed team capacity, which remains source-reported.

## Anonymized Governance Concerns

The public application and vote record raised the following substantive concerns
without retaining voter or reviewer identities:

- whether additional GCC funding has meaningful marginal value given the
  source-reported funding already committed by other organizations;
- whether the contribution to Chinese-speaking developers is concrete enough;
- whether translation alone is an adequate community contribution;
- whether a direct community connection or a defined Chinese-developer output
  should be a clearer commitment;
- whether the two engineering deliverables and their completion evidence will be
  sufficient for milestone acceptance.

One public supporting comment specifically requested a promised contribution to
Chinese-speaking developers. Attribution, wallet data and per-voter metadata are
not retained here.

from translation alone.
