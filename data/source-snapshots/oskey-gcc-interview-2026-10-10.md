## Publicly usable project context

- The interview presents OSKey as a non-commercial, open-source hardware-wallet project focused on hardware sovereignty and user-reproducible devices. The founder says the project wants to serve Ethereum users while reusing existing commercial wallet clients rather than building a full client itself.
- The founder describes the existing workflow as buying a general-purpose development board, compiling OSKey firmware and flashing it to the device. He identifies this setup as difficult for non-technical users.
- The project aims to supply modular firmware, templates and guidance so users can choose device features and form factors with AI assistance. Custom PCB and enclosure design are part of that proposed direction, not an established end-user service.

## Dated progress, reported by the interviewee

- As of the 2026-10-10 interview, the founder said the AI-assisted personalized-hardware path had only reached proof of concept. Code modularization, end-to-end workflow testing, PCB-design improvements and user instructions were still in development.
- The founder hoped users could follow a manual to build a device by late October 2026. This is a forecast in the interview, not evidence that a manual, secure design, completed product or GCC milestone was delivered.
